01 / CHARACTER FILES

GTA 6 Characters

Profiles of Jason Duval, Lucia Caminos and the supporting cast introduced by Rockstar. Find out what is officially known about the people of Leonida.

← BACK TO THE FIELD LIBRARY

THE TWO LEADS

PROTAGONIST / 01

JASON DUVAL

Jason grew up around grifters and crooks. After serving in the Army, he ended up working for local drug runners in the Keys. Rockstar describes him as wanting an easy life, though things keep getting harder. His connection with Lucia becomes central to the story.

PROTAGONIST / 02

LUCIA CAMINOS

Lucia learned to fight from her father. Fighting for her family led to time in Leonida Penitentiary. Recently released, she wants the good life her mother dreamed of and is determined to take matters into her own hands.

THE SUPPORTING CAST

JASON'S CIRCLE

CAL HAMPTON

Jason's friend and an associate of Brian. Cal prefers staying home, listening in on Coast Guard communications and indulging his conspiratorial side.

VICE CITY BUSINESS

BOOBIE IKE

A local legend whose legitimate empire includes real estate, a strip club and a recording studio. He partners with Dre'Quan on Only Raw Records.

MUSIC SCENE

DRE'QUAN PRIEST

A hustler whose ambition is the music business. He works with Boobie and has signed the duo Real Dimez to Only Raw Records.

MUSIC DUO

REAL DIMEZ

Bae-Luxe and Roxy are friends from high school who turned viral music and social media savvy into a route toward fame.

HEIST CONNECTION

RAUL BAUTISTA

A seasoned bank robber who looks for people willing to take the risks behind high-reward scores.

THE KEYS

BRIAN HEDER

An old-school drug runner with a boat yard in the Keys. Jason lives at one of Brian's properties in exchange for helping with local shakedowns.

These summaries are based on Rockstar’s official character descriptions. Performer names below are included only where Rockstar’s credits identify the role; no unannounced plot outcomes or abilities are inferred.
