GTA 6 Launch Guide

The essential launch reference: release platforms, editions, preload information and regional access details, using official Rockstar and PlayStation sources.

← BACK TO THE FIELD LIBRARY

RELEASE INFORMATION

RELEASE DATE

19 NOVEMBER 2026

Rockstar lists Thursday, November 19, 2026.

CONFIRMED CONSOLES

PS5 & XBOX SERIES X|S

These are the platforms listed by Rockstar's official support information.

PRE-LOAD

AVAILABLE FROM NOV 10

Rockstar says digital pre-load began November 10, 2026. PlayStation uses midnight local time; Xbox uses 12 a.m. GMT. PlayStation support specifies at least 212 GB of free storage for PS5.

EDITIONS

STANDARD & ULTIMATE

Rockstar lists Standard and Ultimate editions, plus an Ultimate Edition Upgrade.

INDIA: PLAYSTATION STORE

PlayStation India's listing shows a Standard Edition price of ₹5,999 and an Ultimate Edition price of ₹7,499. Store pricing, offers and availability can change; check the live store before purchasing.

PHYSICAL VERSION: IMPORTANT DETAIL

Rockstar Support describes the physical version as a code-in-box download code, not a disc. Check the exact retailer listing and region compatibility before buying a code. Rockstar says the box contains a download code, not a disc.

XBOX CLOUD GAMING AND REMOTE PLAY

Xbox has announced day-one cloud streaming through Xbox Cloud Gaming on supported Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S consoles. Availability, device eligibility and streaming quality vary by market and connection. Rockstar also lists Remote Play support on PlayStation and Xbox consoles.

PLATFORM & REGION CHECKLIST

  • Confirm your console is PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S.
  • Check that the game code region matches your platform account region.
  • Check available storage and download time before launch.
  • For regional unlock timing, follow Rockstar Support's regional release-time page.

PC: Rockstar's currently listed platforms are PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. We will not claim a PC release date unless Rockstar announces one.
