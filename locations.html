THE STATE OF LEONIDA

Grand Theft Auto VI takes place in Leonida, a fictional state inspired by the Florida setting Rockstar describes as home to modern-day Vice City. Rockstar's official site names six locations. It has not provided a complete official interactive map or confirmed every boundary and route between them.

01 / CITY

VICE CITY

The neon-soaked major city at the heart of Leonida. Rockstar's material also names places within the city such as Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, the Tisha-Wocka flea market and VC Port.

02 / ISLANDS

LEONIDA KEYS

An island region connected to Jason's work and Brian Heder's boat-yard operation.

03 / WETLANDS

GRASSRIVERS

A named region in Rockstar's official Leonida material. Detailed boundaries and a complete location list have not been published there.

04 / COAST

PORT GELLHORN

A named Leonida destination featured in Rockstar's official locations showcase.

05 / INLAND

AMBROSIA

A named destination in the state of Leonida. We will add verified landmarks and activities as they are confirmed.

06 / WILDERNESS

MOUNT KALAGA

Rockstar identifies Mount Kalaga as a national park destination in Leonida.

WHAT WE KNOW ABOUT THE MAP

  • Leonida and the six destination names above are shown in Rockstar's official material.
  • A fan reconstruction, satellite overlay or estimated map size is not an official Rockstar map.
  • We will mark community discoveries separately from officially confirmed geography.
OUR STANDARD: This page distinguishes official Rockstar information from information that has not yet been confirmed. We will update it as we verify details in-game and against reliable sources.
