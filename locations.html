WELCOME TO LEONIDA

Grand Theft Auto VI is set in the fictional state of Leonida, home to Vice City and several contrasting regions. Rockstar's official Only in Leonida showcase names six destinations. Its official media gallery also groups screenshots under location headings, giving us a source-backed way to explore the setting without inventing a complete map.

MAP STATUS: Rockstar has not published a complete, navigable in-game map with confirmed roads, borders, distances or coordinates. Our region guide is a reference index, not a leaked or reconstructed map. Each entry separates published facts from details still to be verified.

THE SIX NAMED REGIONS

01 / MAJOR CITY

VICE CITY

Rockstar describes Leonida as home to the neon-soaked streets of Vice City. The official location material highlights the city as a central destination. Its media gallery has a dedicated Vice City screenshot collection.

Confirmed angle: urban setting and city imagery.

02 / ISLAND REGION

LEONIDA KEYS

Rockstar's character material places Jason in the Keys, where he works for local drug runners. Brian Heder operates a boat yard in the Keys and is part of Jason's story.

Confirmed angle: island setting, Jason's work and Brian's boat-yard operation.

GTA 6 MAP FAQ

How many regions are named in GTA 6?

Rockstar's official location showcase names six destinations: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga.

Is the full GTA 6 map officially available?

The sources linked on this page do not provide a complete navigable map with roads, borders or coordinates. Our schematic is an unofficial visual index, not the in-game map.

Is Vice City the only major location?

No. Rockstar names five additional Leonida destinations alongside Vice City: Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga.

Where can I see official GTA 6 location screenshots?

Rockstar's official media gallery groups images under location labels including Vice City, Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Grassrivers and Mount Kalaga National Park.
