The GTA Guide is an independent fan-made publication covering Grand Theft Auto VI. We organise publicly available information into practical reference pages for players and fans.

← BACK TO THE GUIDE

OUR PURPOSE

We aim to make GTA VI information easier to explore: character profiles, locations, maps, vehicles, missions, launch information and news. Our focus is clarity and usefulness, not publishing the largest volume of speculation.

The GTA Guide is not an official Rockstar Games website, is not endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive, and does not claim access to confidential or unreleased game information.

HOW WE VERIFY INFORMATION

  • Official first: We prioritise Rockstar Games announcements, official game pages, media, and Rockstar Support for facts about the game.
  • Independent reporting: Reputable games journalism may provide context. We identify reporting as reporting and link to the original source.
  • Community discoveries: Player observations, forum posts and social posts are leads, not proof. We label them as community-reported until independently verified.
  • Unknown means unknown: We do not invent cheat codes, mission steps, map coordinates, vehicle statistics, unlock conditions or release details.

GUIDE & WALKTHROUGH STANDARD

Where hands-on verification is required, we distinguish tested information from pre-release material and secondary reporting. Mission guides should identify spoiler level and platform where relevant. If a detail cannot be checked, we say so rather than presenting a guess as a walkthrough.

UPDATES & CORRECTIONS

Pages may change as official information is released or reliable new evidence becomes available. We aim to keep time-sensitive information dated and to correct material errors when identified. A page's update date should not be interpreted as proof that every detail on that page was independently retested on that date.

OUR EDITORIAL PRINCIPLES

  • Accuracy takes priority over speed and search traffic.
  • Sources should be linked close to the claims they support.
  • Confirmed facts, independent reporting and speculation should remain clearly separated.
  • We do not imply a commercial, editorial or licensing relationship with Rockstar Games.
  • We do not sell access to guides or require payment to read this publication.
